## What this benchmark does not show

- Whether the page or video explains the system well: that needs a human or a blind frame-by-frame review, which is not part of this run.
- 1.5.0 output quality on a fixture: 1.5.0 had no palette, kit, diagram planner, layout engine or page builder, so it cannot compose the page or the scenes from a fixture without a model. Its own tools that do exist (quality scorer, claims gate, frame capture with the disk guard) were run on the same inputs.
- Narration timing comes from a fixed words-per-second rate, not a voice; voice sync is measured against those timings.
- Frames per second and wall time depend on the machine and its load at the time of the run.
